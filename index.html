Yes. SA Builder's Licence BLD 271132, fully insured for public liability. You can verify the licence yourself if you want peace of mind.

What areas of Adelaide do you cover?

Adelaide-wide — from the beaches through to the eastern suburbs. If you're not sure whether your suburb's covered, just ask.

Both. I'll do a single ceiling repair or a full room reno — whatever's on your list. No job's too small.

How quickly can you get to a job?

I get back to enquiries promptly and I'm straight with you about timing — if I can't fit you in fast, I'll tell you rather than leave you guessing.

Do you work with property managers and NDIS clients?

Yes. I do rental property maintenance, insurance repairs and end-of-lease work for landlords and property managers, and I'm NDIS welcome for self- or plan-managed clients.
